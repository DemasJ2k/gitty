# Australia setup (do this first)

The whole legal footprint for starting. Budget: an afternoon, roughly A$0.

## 1. ABN as a sole trader

- Apply free at the Australian Business Register (abr.gov.au). Do not use
  third-party sites that charge ~$50–$100 for the same thing.
- Sole trader is the right structure to start: A$0 setup, no annual fee. You
  report business income on your personal tax return.
- You need the ABN to invoice properly (without it, clients must withhold 47%).

## 2. GST: skip it for now

- Registration is only mandatory once turnover reaches **A$75,000/year**. Below
  that it is optional. At $500–$2k/month you are well under, so do not register
  and do not charge GST yet.
- Watch the line: if you approach A$75k, you must register within 21 days.

## 3. Stripe in AUD

- Create a Stripe account for your ABN, set to **AUD**. Use Stripe Invoicing to
  bill retainers, or a simple recurring subscription.
- Stripe supports Australian businesses and 135+ currencies if you ever sell
  abroad (service exports to overseas clients are generally GST-free).

## 4. The rest

- A business email and a one-page site are enough. No company, no accountant
  needed yet.
- Incorporate (Pty Ltd, ~A$611 setup + A$329/year) only later, when liability
  or profit justifies it — not now.
- Declare business income from the first dollar. Keep simple records (a
  spreadsheet of income and expenses is fine at this scale).

## Checklist

- [ ] ABN registered (free, abr.gov.au)
- [ ] Stripe account live, set to AUD
- [ ] Business email set up
- [ ] Simple income/expense sheet started

*General information, not tax advice. Confirm specifics with an Australian
accountant.*
