# Pricing and scope

Offer three tiers. A three-option choice roughly doubles close rate versus a
single price and anchors buyers to the middle. Hold your floor: do not discount
to A$500 to close a nervous first deal. One client at the base tier already
meets your target.

## The three tiers (monthly retainer, AUD, ex-GST while under A$75k)

### Starter — A$1,500/month
For a single-location business that needs to get found.
- Google Business Profile: claim, complete, optimize (categories, services,
  description, photos, hours).
- NAP consistency fix across the key directories AI answers cite.
- AI-search visibility baseline + monthly re-check (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity,
  Google AI Overview) with a short report.
- Structured website content for AI extraction: services, service areas, FAQ.

### Growth — A$2,500/month (most popular)
Everything in Starter, plus ongoing content and reviews.
- 2–4 pieces/month of AI-optimized local content (service/suburb pages, FAQs).
- Review generation workflow + responding to reviews and Q&A.
- Monthly competitor visibility comparison.
- Schema/LocalBusiness markup maintained.

### Scale — A$4,000/month
Everything in Growth, for multi-location or more competitive markets.
- Per-location profiles and content.
- Broader directory/citation footprint.
- Priority turnaround and a monthly strategy call.

Present Starter and Scale, but recommend Growth. Most buyers pick the middle.

## Payment

- Monthly via Stripe (AUD), invoiced in advance. First invoice the day they say
  yes.
- Month-to-month, 30 days notice to cancel. No lock-in makes the first yes
  easier and, per the research, retainers still last far longer than
  project work.

## Written scope boundary (send with every agreement)

This is what keeps a retainer from silently becoming unpaid full-service work.

- **Included:** the deliverables listed in the client's tier, each month.
- **Not included** (quoted separately): website rebuilds, paid ad management,
  logo/branding, social media management, booking-system setup, anything outside
  local search visibility.
- **Reporting:** one monthly report + the re-run audit. Ad-hoc requests beyond
  the tier are booked into next month or quoted.
- **Client provides:** access to their Google Business Profile and website, and
  a single point of contact.
- **Turnaround:** normal changes within [X] business days.

## What you are NOT selling

No trading, no financial products, no investment or "signal" anything. This
service sits entirely in local marketing, which keeps you clear of ASIC's
financial-services licensing regime. Keep it that way in every message.

## Simple close math

- 1 × Growth (A$2,500) = target hit, comfortably.
- 1 × Starter (A$1,500) = target hit.
- Add a second client for buffer against churn (~25%/year), then decide whether
  to grow or hold.
