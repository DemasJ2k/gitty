# Build a 100–150 prospect list (one vertical)

Pick one vertical and stay in it. A single vertical makes your audit, outreach,
and proof compound instead of resetting on every prospect.

## Pick one (decide in a day, do not over-optimize)

Good starting verticals for this service in Australia:

| Vertical | Why it fits | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| **Dental / allied health** (physio, chiro, optometry) | Real marketing budgets, location-based demand, values being found locally | Some franchises handle marketing centrally |
| **Boutique law** (family, conveyancing, wills/estates) | High client value, competitive on search, pays for visibility | Slower to trust a new vendor |
| **Trades** (electricians, plumbers, HVAC, builders) | Huge volume, "near me" search is everything, underserved on AI search | Price-sensitive, less desk time |

Choose based on which you can reach easily (any warm connection wins) and which
you would not mind talking to every week. Then commit.

## Build the list (an afternoon)

Target 100–150 businesses in your vertical, concentrated in a few
suburbs/regions so "near me" queries are comparable.

1. **Google Maps** — search "[vertical] in [suburb]" across 5–10 suburbs. Record
   business name, website, phone, and whether the listing looks complete.
2. **ABN Lookup** (abr.business.gov.au) — confirm the entity and that it is
   active/independent (not a national chain HQ).
3. **Find the decision-maker** — for small clinics/firms/trades this is usually
   the owner or practice manager. Get a name from the website "about" page or
   LinkedIn.
4. **Get a contact route** — email from the site, a contact form, LinkedIn, or
   phone. Two routes per prospect is ideal for follow-up.

## What to capture per prospect (feeds the tracker)

- Business name, suburb, website, phone
- Owner/decision-maker name
- Email + one more contact route (LinkedIn/phone/form)
- One-line audit finding (from `audit-template.md`) — the personalized hook
- Status (see `pipeline-tracker.csv`)

## Quality filters (skip these)

- National franchises with central marketing.
- Businesses with no website and no Google Business Profile at all (harder first
  sale; revisit later).
- Anyone clearly already running a strong local-SEO agency (check their footer).

Aim for prospects that are *doing okay but invisible on AI search* — the gap you
can show them in 30 seconds.
