# Cold outreach sequence (3–7 touches)

Cold outreach converts at roughly one client per 50–100 targeted contacts when
you follow up 3–7 times. Most people quit after one touch, which is why most
people get no clients. The personalized audit finding is what makes this work.

## Ground rules

- Lead with their specific gap, not your service. One verifiable finding per
  prospect (from the audit).
- Keep each message short. You are asking for a 15-minute call, not the sale.
- Space touches ~2–4 days apart. Vary the channel (email, then LinkedIn, then a
  call).
- Track every touch in `pipeline-tracker.csv`. Stop the sequence when they reply
  or after touch 7.

Replace the bracketed parts. Examples use a dentist; swap for your vertical.

## Touch 1 — Email (the gap)

**Subject:** you're missing from AI search in [suburb]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I asked ChatGPT and Google's AI "best dentist in [suburb]" this morning.
> [Competitor A] and [Competitor B] came up. [Their business] didn't.
>
> More locals are asking AI instead of scrolling Google, and right now those
> searches are being handed to your competitors.
>
> I put together a one-page audit showing exactly where you're missing and why.
> Want me to send it over? No charge.
>
> [Your name]
> [ABN] · [phone]

## Touch 2 — Email (deliver the audit), ~3 days later

**Subject:** your AI visibility audit (1 page)

> Hi [Name], here's the audit I mentioned — attached.
>
> The short version: your Google Business Profile is missing [specific thing],
> and you're not showing up when locals ask AI tools for a dentist nearby.
> Both are fixable.
>
> I help [vertical] practices in [region] fix this and stay visible. Worth a
> quick 15-minute call this week? Here's my calendar: [link]
>
> [Your name]

## Touch 3 — LinkedIn connect + note, ~3 days later

> Hi [Name] — sent you an AI-visibility audit for [business] by email. Reached
> out because [competitor] is getting the AI-search traffic you're missing in
> [suburb]. Happy to walk you through the fixes if useful.

## Touch 4 — Email (a second concrete proof point), ~4 days later

**Subject:** one more thing on [business]'s listing

> Hi [Name], quick follow-up. Beyond AI search, your Google Business Profile has
> [second specific gap, e.g. "no posts in 6 months and 40 fewer reviews than
> [competitor]"]. That's costing you the "near me" clicks too.
>
> I can fix both and keep them fixed. 15 minutes to show you how? [link]

## Touch 5 — Phone call, ~3 days later

> Hi [Name], it's [Your name] — I sent through an AI-visibility audit for
> [business]. Did you get a chance to look? Happy to walk you through the two
> quick wins in a couple of minutes now, or book a proper time.

Keep it light. If they are busy, ask for a better time. A call often unsticks a
thread that email did not.

## Touch 6 — Email (break-up), ~4 days later

**Subject:** should I close your file?

> Hi [Name], I don't want to keep landing in your inbox. If AI-search visibility
> isn't a priority right now, no problem — just let me know and I'll leave it.
>
> If it is, the audit still stands and the fixes take me about a month. [link]

## Touch 7 — Optional, ~2 weeks later

A single value-add: a short note when something changes (a Google update, a new
competitor outranking them, a fresh audit finding). Then stop.

## When they reply

Book the 15-minute call. On the call: walk through the audit, confirm the gap
matters to them, then present the tiers from `pricing-and-scope.md`. Hold your
price. Send a Stripe invoice the same day they say yes.
