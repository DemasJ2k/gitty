# GEO/AEO Service Starter Kit (Australia)

Everything you need to land your first Australian SMB retainer client for an
**AI-search visibility (GEO/AEO) + Google Business Profile** service. Built from
the council decision in
[`../2026-australia-buy-vs-build-decision.md`](../2026-australia-buy-vs-build-decision.md).

The goal: one client at **A$1,500+/month** clears your $500–$2k target. This kit
gets you from zero to a sendable pitch fast, using only off-the-shelf tools
(<A$100/month).

## The service in one line

You make sure a local business shows up when its customers ask ChatGPT, Gemini,
Perplexity, and Google ("best dentist near Bondi", "conveyancer in Newcastle")
and when they look at its Google Business Profile — and you keep it that way for
a monthly fee.

## The one rule that makes or breaks this

You are technical, so your instinct will be to build a tool. Do not. The failure
mode is building instead of selling. Hit your **weekly outbound quota before you
touch any code**. Zero conversations at week two is the real failure signal, not
zero revenue.

## Not advice

This kit is general information for a marketing-services business, not legal,
tax, or financial advice. Confirm ABN/GST/structure with an Australian
accountant. This service deliberately stays clear of financial-product territory
(no trading, no signals, no securities) so ASIC's licensing rules do not apply.
