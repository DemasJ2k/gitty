# The AI Visibility Audit

Your outreach hook and your first deliverable. Run it per prospect in ~10
minutes. The point is a specific, verifiable gap you can show them: "When I ask
ChatGPT for the best [vertical] in [suburb], you don't come up. Three of your
competitors do."

## What you check

### 1. Generative search presence (the differentiator)
Ask each of these, as a normal customer would, and record whether the business
appears and who does:

- ChatGPT (with web/search on): "best [vertical] in [suburb]?"
- Google Gemini: same query
- Perplexity: same query
- Google AI Overview: run the query in Google, note the AI answer box

Record: does the prospect appear? Which competitors appear instead? What
sources do the AI answers cite (directories, review sites, the competitor's own
pages)?

### 2. Google Business Profile (the quick win)
- Is the profile claimed and complete (hours, services, description, categories)?
- Photos recent? Posts in the last month?
- Review count and average vs the top 3 competitors in the area.
- Are they answering reviews and Q&A?

### 3. Website signals that feed AI answers
- Is there clear, structured info AI can extract (services, location, FAQs)?
- Any schema markup (LocalBusiness)? (Quick check: is it in the page source, or
  use a free schema validator.)
- Does the site plainly state the suburbs/areas they serve?

### 4. Directory and review footprint
- Listed and consistent on the directories your AI answers cited (name, address,
  phone matching everywhere)?
- Present on the review sites AI tends to pull from?

## The one-page audit you send

Keep it to a single page. Structure:

1. **The headline gap** (one sentence): "You are invisible when locals ask AI
   for a [vertical] in [suburb]. Here is the proof."
2. **Screenshots**: the AI answers that name competitors, not them.
3. **Google Business Profile gaps**: 3–5 concrete misses vs a named competitor.
4. **What it costs them**: framed simply — these are customers actively asking
   for a business like theirs and being sent elsewhere.
5. **The fix**: "This is fixable. I do it for local [vertical] businesses. 15
   minutes to walk you through it?"

## Turning the audit into the deliverable

When they sign, the audit becomes month one's work plan: claim/complete the
profile, fix NAP consistency, add structured service/area/FAQ content, build the
directory/review footprint AI answers cite, then re-run the audit monthly to
show movement. The monthly re-run is what justifies the retainer.

## Tooling (all cheap or free)

- The AI tools themselves (free tiers are enough to run queries).
- Google Business Profile (free) — the core deliverable.
- A free schema validator for the website check.
- Optional, only after you have a paying client: a GEO tracking tool
  (~A$29–80/mo, e.g. Otterly.ai) to automate the monthly re-check. Do not buy it
  before you have revenue.
